Cleaning Decision Summary

October 21, 2026 · Portfolio edition · Simulated scenario

Checking duplicates

I reviewed the 36 source rows and kept 32 contacts after checking four duplicate pairs. I matched names, email addresses, companies, and notes before merging, kept the original record IDs, and retained later dates only when another source record supported them.

Fixing what the source supports

I standardized clear formatting issues. I left missing emails, ambiguous statuses, and conflicting dates for review. Sam Villanueva stayed separate from Ben Cruz because the shared email did not establish that they were the same person.

Preparing the handoff

I separated the 20 ready contacts from the 12 needing review before the HubSpot import. I kept the spreadsheet lead stages in a custom property so they would not be confused with HubSpot’s default fields. I then created four follow-up tasks with the recorded due dates and notes. The tasks remain Not Started.
